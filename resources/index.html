Resources

Small tools for a useful next step.

These original guides are available now. Use them independently, with a parent or guardian, or with an educator.

✦
GuideCareer exploration

Five-step route

Explore a career without guessing.

  1. Notice: Write down tasks and topics that hold your attention.
  2. Research: Compare daily work, training routes, environments, and tradeoffs.
  3. Ask: Speak with someone who can share real examples.
  4. Try: Choose a small project, course, event, or volunteer task.
  5. Reflect: Record what changed and what you want to explore next.
Prompt setProfessional conversation

Four better questions

Ask about the work behind the title.

  • What surprised you when you entered this field?
  • Which skill do beginners underestimate?
  • What does a normal week actually look like?
  • What is one low-risk way to test my interest?
ReflectionLeadership experience

Make experience specific

Describe what you did, not only your title.

Write down the situation, your responsibility, the action you took, and what changed. Separate the team’s result from your own contribution. Then identify one choice you would repeat and one you would change.

Use these as educational tools—not promises.
