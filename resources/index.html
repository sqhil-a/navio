Career Clarity resource library

Practical guides students can use today.

Each guide teaches one part of careful career exploration with a repeatable method, a worked example, and a direct next action.

Three skill guides

Move from a claim to evidence, then to action.

The guides follow the same sequence used in the Career Clarity Program. Begin by judging the quality of career information. Use the evidence to prepare a focused conversation. Then design a small activity that tests one part of the work.

Guide 1 · About 15 minutes

Evaluate career information

Learn a five-part test for authority, date, location, evidence, and purpose. Build a claim log that separates legal requirements, common preferences, and personal experience.

  • Source ladder for Ontario career research
  • Worked claim-check example
  • Warning signs and a reusable note format
Use the source guide

Guide 2 · About 20 minutes

Plan a professional conversation

Turn an unanswered research question into a respectful request, five useful questions, and a safe follow-up. The guide includes a message template and boundaries for youth communication.

  • Preparation and outreach steps
  • Question bank organized by purpose
  • Fact-check and thank-you process
Prepare a conversation

Guide 3 · About 20 minutes

Design an experience test

Test one career assumption through a safe, limited activity instead of treating a large commitment as an experiment. Choose a question, task, evidence, boundary, and reflection date.

  • Five-part test plan
  • Examples across several fields
  • Reflection method for deciding what comes next
Design an experience test
