Resources

Preparation tools for the work behind the pitch.

Programs are where participants apply their skills. These concise guides support that experience by making case analysis, recommendation building, and presentation structure easier to practise.

✦
Resources are supporting material.

They do not replace the competition experience, guarantee an outcome, or serve as a closed application. Use them to prepare, practise, and ask better questions.

Tool 01CASE / READ

Case analysis

Find the decision inside the details.

Before suggesting a solution, write one sentence for each of the following. If the case does not provide an answer, label your assumption instead of presenting it as fact.

  1. Organization: What does the business offer, to whom, and how does it create value?
  2. Decision: What choice must be made now, and who is responsible for making it?
  3. Evidence: Which facts, numbers, customer insights, or constraints materially affect that choice?
  4. Success: What would a strong outcome look like, and how could the organization tell?
Tool 02CASE / BUILD

Recommendation test

Make the idea survive contact with reality.

A recommendation is stronger when it answers more than “what.” Test your direction through five practical lenses before building slides.

  • Fit: Does it solve the problem the case actually presents?
  • Evidence: Which case facts support it, and which assumptions need to be stated?
  • Feasibility: What people, money, time, or capabilities does it require?
  • Risk: What could fail, and what would reduce that risk?
  • Measurement: Which early result would show that the plan is working?
Tool 03CASE / PITCH

Presentation structure

Lead the audience through the decision.

A clear pitch is a sequence of reasoning, not a stack of decorated slides. Open with the decision, show why it matters, and make every section earn its place.

OpenName the problem and recommendation.

ProveUse the case evidence that changes the decision.

PlanExplain actions, ownership, timing, and resources.

ProtectAddress risks, tradeoffs, and measurement.

CloseReturn to the result your plan is built to create.
