Available by arrangement

Guided use for a school or community group.

An educator or community organization may contact Navio to discuss facilitating the public toolkit with an Ontario secondary-student group. The inquiry is a planning conversation, not an open public registration or a promise of delivery.

Before confirming a guided activity, Navio and the requesting organization document the audience, purpose, facilitator, timing, online or in-person format, supervision, accessibility, privacy, youth-safety responsibilities, and communication process.

Email a group request →

How listings are approved

Details before promotion.

Before Navio marks an application-based opportunity open, an accountable owner confirms its purpose, audience, format or location, dates, cost, capacity, responsibilities, accessibility, privacy, supervision, consent requirements, destination, and contact process. Closed or changed listings are updated promptly.

This policy helps prevent students from acting on sample content, old deadlines, or opportunities that are still being explored internally.
