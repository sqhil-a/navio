About Navio

A clearer way to think about what comes next.

Navio Pathways helps young people explore careers, contribution, and leadership before they are expected to have every answer.

✦
Our purpose

Our mission

Make the future easier to explore.

We create practical guidance and community-connected learning that helps youth understand options, ask better questions, contribute meaningfully, and choose informed next steps.

Navio is not a school, placement agency, or promise of a particular outcome. It is an Ontario not-for-profit built to help young people approach important decisions with more context and confidence.

SA

Founder and President

Sahil Ambegaonkar

Sahil created Navio Pathways after seeing how often students are expected to make major career decisions without enough real-world context, structure, or encouragement. He leads the organization’s direction, public resources, and community outreach.

How we work

Three standards for useful guidance.

Begin with the real question.

We focus on what a young person is trying to understand—not the answer adults assume they need.

Connect reflection to action.

A resource should lead to a useful conversation, comparison, experiment, or next step.

Be exact about what exists.

We distinguish available resources and contact routes from ideas that have not been launched.
