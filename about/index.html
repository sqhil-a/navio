About Navio Pathways

Helping students explore before they are expected to decide.

Navio Pathways is an Ontario incorporated not-for-profit that creates practical career-awareness experiences and resources for secondary students.

Our mission

Give young people a clearer way to investigate their future.

Career exploration should be more than choosing a title from a list. Our mission is to help Ontario youth understand the questions behind a career decision: What does the work involve? Which education or training routes lead there? What skills matter? What tradeoffs should a student consider? What small experience could test an interest before a major commitment?

Navio Pathways turns those questions into guided workshops and freely available resources. Students leave with a process they can repeat as their interests, circumstances, and opportunities change.

Our work

Career education connected to practical inquiry.

01

Workshop design

We organize career exploration into research, questions, reflection, and a realistic next step.

02

Youth guidance

We explain pathways in plain language without assuming that students already know how education and work connect.

03

Professional perspectives

Guest conversations focus on the realities of work rather than promotion or recruitment.

How we operate

Clear claims, careful boundaries, and current information.

Navio publishes a session only after its date, format, audience, and registration process are confirmed. We do not invent partner names, speaker biographies, participant numbers, testimonials, or impact statistics. When something changes, the workshop page and dated updates are the public source of truth.

Because the organization serves youth, privacy and safety are part of program design. Registration collects only the information needed to administer a session. Communication uses approved channels, accessibility requests are handled without requiring a diagnosis, and concerns can be reported through the organization’s monitored email.

Navio is not a school, placement agency, tutoring business, or employment service. Its workshops and resources provide general education and do not replace advice from an educator, counsellor, regulated professional, or parent or guardian.

Leadership

Sahil Ambegaonkar

Sahil is the Founder and President of Navio Pathways. He established the organization after seeing how often students are expected to make significant education and career decisions without enough context, structure, or opportunities to ask practical questions.
