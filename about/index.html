About Navio Pathways

Practical career education for Ontario youth.

Navio Pathways is an Ontario incorporated not-for-profit organization serving secondary students through the free Career Clarity Program and public career resources.

Mission

Help young people investigate before they commit.

Students are often asked to choose a direction before they understand daily work, education routes, or the tradeoffs behind a career title. Navio Pathways gives students a repeatable process for turning uncertainty into research, experience, and a realistic next step.

Our work connects career exploration with practical questions about education, volunteering, leadership, skills, and community contribution.

Current program

Navio Career Clarity Program

A free five-stage program for Ontario students in Grades 9–12. Participants build a Career Clarity Portfolio containing personal work criteria, verified career evidence, a two-route pathway comparison, an experience test, and a 30-day plan.

Public resources

Evidence-first guidance

Navio publishes HTML guides that help students assess sources, prepare professional questions, and test an interest safely. The program and resources are available without an account or fee.
