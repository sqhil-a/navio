About Navio Pathways

Practical career education for Ontario youth.

Navio Pathways is an Ontario incorporated not-for-profit organization serving secondary students through the free Career Clarity Program and public career resources.

Mission

Help young people investigate before they commit.

Students are often asked to choose a direction before they understand daily work, education routes, or the tradeoffs behind a career title. Navio Pathways gives students a repeatable process for turning uncertainty into research, experience, and a realistic next step.

Our work connects career exploration with practical questions about education, volunteering, leadership, skills, and community contribution.

Current program

Navio Career Clarity Program

A free five-stage program for Ontario students in Grades 9–12. Students build a Career Clarity Portfolio containing personal work criteria, verified career evidence, a two-route pathway comparison, an experience test, and a 30-day plan.

The program is open now as a self-paced public learning experience. It takes about 2.5 hours, requires no registration, and does not collect workbook answers.

Public resources

Evidence-first guidance

Navio publishes detailed HTML guides that help students assess sources, prepare professional questions, and test an interest safely. A complete fictional portfolio shows how the assignments fit together, and a facilitator guide helps adults introduce the process.

How we work

One public program with clear boundaries.

Navio's current operating model is direct publication: we design, maintain, and provide the Career Clarity Program, workbook, career-research guides, worked example, and facilitator material through this website. Students complete the work independently or with an appropriate adult. Questions and corrections use the organization's monitored public email.

We do not describe website visits as program completions, publish unsupported participant totals, promise outcomes, sell student information, or present the program as individualized counselling. The program does not offer a credential, school credit, placement, or volunteer hours.

Service area and audience

Designed for Ontario secondary students.

Examples point students toward responsible Ontario and Canadian sources because admissions, credentials, regulation, and labour information depend on location and date. Students in Grades 9–12 are the primary audience; educators, guidance staff, youth workers, parents, and guardians can use the facilitator guide.

The learning method may still be useful elsewhere, but users must replace Ontario sources and requirements with the authorities responsible in their own location.

Content stewardship

Sources and corrections matter.

Program pages show a publication or review date. Guidance distinguishes official requirements from common preferences and personal accounts. We link students to current government, regulator, and institution sources rather than copying changing admissions or labour figures into static claims.

Navio reviews program material when a correction is reported or the workflow changes. Readers can email the exact page, statement, and supporting source to hello@naviopathways.com.
