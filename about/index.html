About Navio

Experience should arrive before the big decision.

Navio Pathways creates practical programs where young people can test interests, build useful skills, and meet the kinds of problems that careers are actually made of.

✦
Our mission

Turn career curiosity into something young people can do.

Students are asked to choose courses, post-secondary paths, and career directions before they have had many chances to experience real professional thinking. Navio closes part of that gap through structured challenges, practical preparation, and community participation.

Our mission is to help youth explore careers and build confidence through experiences that require judgment, communication, teamwork, and reflection. We do not promise a perfect path. We create a stronger place to begin.

How the organization works

Experience, preparation, and community—working together.

Each part has a different role, so young people can understand what to do and why it matters.

Sahil Ambegaonkar
