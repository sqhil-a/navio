The program

Finish with evidence, not another personality quiz.

Students choose one career question and complete five assignments: define personal work criteria, research the work through three reliable sources, compare two pathways, plan a low-risk experience test, and decide on a next step.

The completed assignments form a Career Clarity Portfolio that can support a conversation with a parent, educator, guidance counsellor, or professional.

See the stages and completion requirements →

What students produce

Five useful pieces of work.

01

Career criteria

A ranked list of tasks, environments, values, and constraints that matter.

02

Evidence brief

Verified notes on daily work, skills, outlook, requirements, and tradeoffs.

03

Pathway comparison

A side-by-side comparison of two education or training routes.

04

Experience test

A safe, small activity and five questions that test part of the interest.

05

30-day plan

Three dated next actions and a date to review what was learned.

Our mission

Make career exploration understandable and practical.

Navio Pathways helps young people investigate education, work, volunteering, and leadership before making a major commitment. We turn a broad question—“What should I do?”—into smaller research and reflection tasks a student can complete.

The Career Clarity Program and its workbook are available at no cost and do not require an account or the submission of personal information.
