Our mission

Give young people more context before the big decision.

Course choices, post-secondary pathways, careers, and leadership opportunities arrive before most young people have seen enough of the world behind them. Navio creates clearer ways to explore: practical programs, useful resources, community perspectives, and experiences that turn curiosity into action.

What Navio does

Three ways to make exploration more useful.

Each part of Navio has a different purpose. Together, they help youth move from uncertainty to a practical next step.

Most popular program

Meet the Navio Pathways Case Competition.

NPCC is one expression of Navio’s broader mission: give young people a practical setting to explore a career area and show what they can do.

NPCC // Annual

A real business challenge. A recommendation your team can defend.

Young people interested in finance, accounting, marketing, entrepreneurship, and strategy analyze a case, build a solution, present their thinking, and compete for prizes.
