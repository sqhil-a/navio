Our mission

Make career exploration understandable, practical, and less intimidating.

Students are often asked what they want to become before they have had enough opportunities to understand how different fields actually work. Navio Pathways gives young people a method for exploring those questions. We focus on the decisions that come before an application: recognizing interests, comparing pathways, speaking with professionals, testing assumptions, and choosing a useful next step.

Our work is educational and exploratory. We do not promise admission, employment, volunteer hours, or a particular career outcome. Instead, we help students build the questions and habits that support more informed choices.

Career-awareness workshop

A structured starting point for Ontario students.

The online workshop helps secondary students move from a broad interest to a practical exploration plan. Students learn how to compare roles, education routes, work environments, and skill requirements; prepare questions for a professional; and choose one low-risk action to continue learning.

  • Designed for students in Grades 9–12 in Ontario
  • Delivered online and offered at no cost
  • Includes guided activities and space for questions
  • Uses confirmed session details before registration opens
Program details and schedule

Public benefit

Useful knowledge without a sales pitch.

Navio Pathways is organized around youth education and community benefit. The website contains no advertising, affiliate links, paid placement offers, or commercial lead generation. Core career resources are available in HTML at no charge, so students, families, and educators can use them without downloading a file or creating an account.

Workshop speakers contribute practical perspective. Their participation does not constitute an endorsement, partnership, job offer, or individualized career advice.
