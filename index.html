The main event

Business is learned by making decisions.

NPCC places students inside a realistic business scenario and asks them to decide what the organization should do next—not repeat a definition from a textbook.

NPCC // Annual

Navio Pathways Case Competition

Participants examine a case, identify the strongest issue, compare possible responses, and present a recommendation. The experience connects finance, accounting, marketing, entrepreneurship, and strategy in one practical challenge.

What participants practice

Skills that move from the case room to the real world.

01

Find the signal

Separate the core business problem from the background information and competing priorities.

02

Make the call

Choose a direction, support it with evidence, and understand the tradeoffs behind the decision.

03

Bring people with you

Divide work, combine perspectives, build a concise story, and present it with confidence.
