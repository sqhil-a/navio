Youth safety

Clear boundaries support meaningful learning.

Navio Pathways sets expectations for online workshops, communication, professional guests, privacy, and reporting concerns.

Core expectations

  • Treat every young person and participant with dignity and respect.
  • Use transparent, approved organizational communication channels.
  • Collect only information needed to administer the activity.
  • Keep workshops moderated and maintain clear roles and boundaries.
  • Do not arrange unapproved private contact between a student and a guest speaker.
  • Act on concerns and prohibit retaliation for a good-faith report.

Online workshop boundaries

Meeting access details are shared only with registered participants and must not be redistributed. Participants may not record, photograph, or publish another person’s contribution without permission. Guest speakers answer general, moderated questions and are not expected to provide private mentoring, employment opportunities, personal contact information, or individualized advice.

Communication

Workshop information is sent through the contact route approved for the session. A young person should not be pressured to move a conversation to a private account or share unnecessary personal details. Concerning or unexpected contact should be saved where safe to do so and reported promptly.

Parent or guardian involvement

A confirmed listing will state any consent or participation requirements. Students and families should review the format together and contact Navio before registration if they have a safety or supervision question.

Report a concern

Email hello@naviopathways.com with “Youth safety concern” in the subject line. Include what happened, when it occurred, the program involved, and a safe way to respond. Avoid sending unrelated sensitive information. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first; email is not an emergency channel.

Response

Navio will limit access to a report, document actions appropriately, and take reasonable steps based on the nature and urgency of the concern. Those steps may include preserving information, restricting participation, contacting a parent or guardian where appropriate, escalating to the responsible authority, or seeking qualified advice.
