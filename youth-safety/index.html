Youth safety

Clear adult oversight and communication boundaries.

These expectations apply to NPCC registration, case communication, online event participation, panel interaction, and follow-up.

Adult advisor

Every team names an adult advisor who supports attendance, receives official program communication, and helps the team follow school, family, privacy, and safety requirements. The advisor does not complete the team's competition work.

Official communication

Navio uses hello@naviopathways.com and the advisor's registered email for program administration. Communication involving a student should include the advisor or another appropriate adult. Participants should not be asked to move to unexpected private accounts or provide personal contact details unrelated to the event.

Online event

Participants use the event access route supplied to the advisor, keep display names appropriate, and follow moderator instructions. No participant may record, privately message, harass, threaten, or expose personal information about another person. A team should immediately tell its advisor or event moderator about a concerning interaction.

Panel and volunteer boundaries

Judges and event contributors evaluate the case work within the scheduled program. Participation does not include private mentoring, recruitment, employment, unsupervised meetings, or continued personal access to a student.

Report a concern

Email hello@naviopathways.com with “NPCC 2026 youth safety concern” in the subject line. Include what occurred, when, the event activity involved, and a safe response route. Email is not an emergency channel; contact local emergency services if someone is in immediate danger.
