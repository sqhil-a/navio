Youth safety

Career exploration should use clear boundaries.

These expectations apply when students use the program, contact Navio, or plan a conversation or experience test involving another person.

Public program

The program and workbook require no account and do not submit a student's written answers to Navio. Students should keep personal reflections private and avoid placing sensitive information in messages.

Professional conversations

Use public or appropriately supervised communication. Involve a parent, guardian, educator, or trusted adult where appropriate. A student should never feel pressured to share private contact details, move to an unexpected account, meet alone, accept an offer, or disclose personal information.

Experience tests

Choose a safe, age-appropriate scope. A volunteer task, observation, visit, or conversation should follow the responsible school, organization, workplace, and parent or guardian requirements.

Organization communication

Navio uses its public email and official social profiles. Career learning does not include unapproved private mentoring, recruitment, employment, or private contact between a student and a contributor.

Report a concern

Email hello@naviopathways.com with “Youth safety concern” in the subject line. Include what happened, when, the activity involved, and a safe way to respond. Email is not an emergency channel; contact local emergency services if someone is in immediate danger.
