Youth safety

Clear boundaries support meaningful learning.

Navio Pathways sets expectations for resource use, communication, professional conversations, privacy, and reporting concerns.

Core expectations

  • Treat every young person and contributor with dignity and respect.
  • Use transparent, approved organizational communication channels.
  • Collect only information needed for an approved activity.
  • Maintain clear roles, supervision, and boundaries.
  • Do not arrange unapproved private contact between a student and a professional contributor.
  • Act on concerns and prohibit retaliation for a good-faith report.

Using the public toolkit

The toolkit requires no account and does not submit a student’s written responses to Navio. Students should keep personal reflections private and avoid placing sensitive information in messages to the organization.

Professional conversations

Students should use public or appropriately supervised channels, involve a trusted adult where appropriate, and never feel pressured to share private contact details, move a conversation to an unexpected account, meet alone, accept an offer, or disclose personal information. A professional conversation is for general learning, not private mentoring, recruitment, or employment.

Communication

Organizational communication uses approved public contact routes. Concerning or unexpected contact should be saved where safe to do so and reported promptly. Students and families may contact Navio before participating in any separately arranged activity if they have a safety or supervision question.

Report a concern

Email hello@naviopathways.com with “Youth safety concern” in the subject line. Include what happened, when it occurred, the activity involved, and a safe way to respond. Avoid sending unrelated sensitive information. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first; email is not an emergency channel.

Response

Navio will limit access to a report, document actions appropriately, and take reasonable steps based on the nature and urgency of the concern. Those steps may include preserving information, restricting participation, contacting a parent or guardian where appropriate, escalating to the responsible authority, or seeking qualified advice.
