Privacy notice

Collect only what NPCC needs to operate.

This notice covers the website, team registration, event administration, online competition, direct email, and official social profiles.

Website use

The public website does not require an account and site analytics is currently disabled. Reading competition information does not submit personal information to Navio.

Registration information

Teams register through direct email. Navio requests a team name, student first names and grades, school or municipality, adult advisor name and contact information, and any accessibility or communication request the sender chooses to provide. Do not send birth dates, home addresses, student numbers, identification documents, payment information, or medical records.

How information is used

Navio uses registration information to verify eligibility, confirm the team, communicate with the adult advisor, release the case, schedule presentations, administer the competition, provide accessibility support, resolve concerns, and send evaluation feedback.

Online event

The competition uses an online meeting platform shared with confirmed advisors. Participant display names and presentation content are visible to the people in the relevant event session. Navio does not record a session unless it first provides a specific purpose and obtains the required participant and adult consent.

Access and retention

Access is limited to people administering the event or responding to a concern. Registration and event correspondence is retained only as long as needed for program administration, safety, reporting, organizational records, and applicable legal responsibilities.

Privacy requests

To request access, correction, or deletion where applicable, email hello@naviopathways.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line.
