Privacy notice

The public program does not collect student answers.

This notice covers the website, Career Clarity Program and workbook, direct email, and official social profiles.

Website and program use

You can read the site, complete the program, and use the workbook without an account or form submission. The workbook is static HTML; Navio does not receive answers written in your notebook or private document. Site analytics is currently disabled.

Direct contact

If you email Navio or contact an official social profile, the relevant service processes the information you choose to send. Navio uses it to respond to the request, correct a resource, address accessibility or safety, or administer an approved organizational conversation.

What not to send

Do not send medical records, a diagnosis, government identification, financial information, passwords, or unrelated sensitive information. Navio does not sell contact information or build commercial advertising profiles from inquiries.

Access and retention

Access to correspondence is limited to people who need it to respond or address a concern. Correspondence is retained only as long as needed for the request, organizational administration, safety, reporting, and applicable legal responsibilities.

Privacy requests

To request access, correction, or deletion where applicable, email hello@naviopathways.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line.
