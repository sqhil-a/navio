Privacy notice

Collect what is needed and explain why.

This notice covers the public website, direct email, and the workshop registration workflow when registration is open.

Website use

You can read the public website and career resources without creating an account. Analytics remains disabled unless Navio approves a documented purpose and updates this notice to identify the service, data, storage, retention, and available controls.

Workshop registration

When a confirmed workshop opens, the registration page may collect the student’s name, contact email, grade, selected session, an optional accessibility request, and acknowledgement of the published notices. Navio uses this information to administer the session, communicate access or schedule information, arrange requested support, maintain appropriate participation records, and respond to a concern.

Do not submit a diagnosis, medical record, government identifier, financial information, password, or unrelated sensitive information. If a session requires parent or guardian involvement, that requirement will be stated in the confirmed listing.

Direct contact

If you email Navio or contact an official social account, the relevant service processes the information you choose to send. Email is preferred for detailed organizational requests. Navio does not sell contact information or use workshop registration to create commercial advertising profiles.

Service providers and access

A registration endpoint is enabled only after Navio approves the receiving service and its access, security, retention, and deletion process. Information is limited to people who need it to administer the workshop or respond to a concern. A provider may process data under its own terms and security practices.

Retention and requests

Registration information should be retained only as long as needed for program administration, safety, reporting, and applicable legal responsibilities, then deleted or de-identified according to the approved internal schedule. To ask what information Navio has about you, request a correction or deletion where applicable, or raise a privacy concern, email hello@naviopathways.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line.

Youth privacy

Young people should involve a parent, guardian, educator, or other trusted adult when they are unsure whether information is appropriate to share. Navio does not use the public workshop to arrange private communication between a student and a guest speaker.
