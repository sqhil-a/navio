Privacy notice

Collect what is needed and explain why.

This notice covers the public website, self-guided toolkit, direct email, and official social profiles.

Website and toolkit use

You can read the public website, complete the career toolkit, and use the career resources without creating an account or submitting your answers. Navio does not receive what you write in a notebook or private document. Analytics remains disabled unless Navio approves a documented purpose and updates this notice to identify the service, data, storage, retention, and available controls.

Direct contact

If you email Navio or contact an official social account, the relevant service processes the information you choose to send. Navio may use it to understand and respond to your request, arrange an approved organizational conversation, address accessibility or safety, or maintain necessary correspondence.

Do not submit a diagnosis, medical record, government identifier, financial information, password, or unrelated sensitive information. Navio does not sell contact information or create commercial advertising profiles from inquiries.

Service providers and access

Email and social platforms operate under their own terms and security practices. Access to organizational correspondence is limited to people who need it to respond, administer an approved activity, or address a concern.

Retention and requests

Correspondence should be retained only as long as needed for the request, organizational administration, safety, reporting, and applicable legal responsibilities, then deleted or de-identified according to the approved internal schedule. To ask what information Navio has about you, request a correction or deletion where applicable, or raise a privacy concern, email hello@naviopathways.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line.

Youth privacy

Young people should involve a parent, guardian, educator, or other trusted adult when they are unsure whether information is appropriate to share. Navio does not use its public toolkit to arrange private communication between a student and a professional contributor.
