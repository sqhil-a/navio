Get involved

Contribute practical knowledge to youth career exploration.

Navio welcomes conversations with professionals, educators, community organizations, and responsible volunteers whose experience aligns with its career-awareness mission.

Ways to contribute

Start with a defined purpose.

01

Career speaker

Share the realities of your field through a moderated, educational conversation.

02

School or community contact

Discuss whether a career-awareness workshop fits a group of Ontario secondary students.

03

Resource reviewer

Offer subject-matter feedback on a career guide without promoting a commercial service.

04

Organizational volunteer

Describe the skills and availability you could contribute to program operations.

Career speakers

What a useful contribution looks like.

Speakers should be prepared to discuss daily responsibilities, education or training routes, early-career skills, misconceptions, work environments, challenges, and low-risk ways a student can explore the field. The purpose is education—not recruitment, sales, collecting leads, or arranging private contact with students.

Participation is confirmed only after Navio reviews the topic, format, boundaries, and permission to publish any name or biography.

Schools and community groups

What to include in your message.

Tell us about your organization, the student audience and grade range, approximate group size, preferred timing, accessibility considerations, and the career questions students are asking. A conversation does not confirm delivery; Navio will document the agreed format, responsibilities, supervision, and communication process before announcing a session.

Volunteer expectations

Reliability and youth-safe conduct come first.

Volunteers must communicate through approved channels, protect confidential information, respect program boundaries, avoid unsupported claims, complete any role-appropriate screening or training, and report concerns promptly. Navio does not promise volunteer-hour recognition unless a specific approved role states how hours are supervised and verified.

Emailing an expression of interest does not guarantee a position. Navio confirms only roles that have a defined need, accountable supervisor, responsibilities, schedule, and safe operating process.
