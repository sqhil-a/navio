NPCC 2026 registration · Open

Register your team by November 1.

Registration is free and completed through Navio Pathways' monitored organization email. Review the checklist, prepare the required details, and send one message for the team.

Before registering

Confirm all five requirements.

  • Every student is enrolled in Grade 9, 10, 11, or 12.
  • Every student lives in or attends school in Ontario.
  • The team has two to four students.
  • One adult has agreed to serve as team advisor.
  • The team can participate online on November 15.

What to send

One message with limited information.

  • Team name
  • Each student's first name and grade
  • School name or Ontario municipality
  • Advisor's name, role, and email address
  • Accessibility or communication request, if applicable

Do not send birth dates, student numbers, home addresses, identification documents, medical records, or payment information.

Registration steps

  1. Choose one person to send the team message.
  2. Use the button below to open a pre-addressed email to hello@naviopathways.com.
  3. Complete every listed field and send the message by November 1 at 11:59 p.m. ET.
  4. Navio checks team size, grade, Ontario connection, advisor information, and event availability.
  5. The advisor receives confirmation or a specific correction request within three business days.
Open registration emailAsk a registration question
If the email button does not open: write to hello@naviopathways.com with the subject “NPCC 2026 team registration” and include the five items listed above.

What confirmation means

A confirmation email reserves the team's place, identifies the adult advisor as the official contact, and provides the online event access process. The case is released to confirmed teams on November 9 at 5:00 p.m. ET. A message sent to Navio is not a completed registration until the team receives confirmation.

Changes and withdrawal

The advisor may request a student-name correction, team-member change, accessibility arrangement, or withdrawal by emailing the same inbox. Team changes must continue to satisfy eligibility and team-size rules. Notify Navio promptly if the team cannot attend so event scheduling remains accurate.

Privacy and youth safety

Navio uses registration information to verify eligibility, administer the event, communicate with the advisor, provide accessibility support, and document the competition. Official communication involving students uses the advisor or another appropriate adult. Read the privacy notice and youth-safety approach before sending information.
