What NPCC is

One case. Several business lenses. A recommendation you can defend.

NPCC places participants in a realistic business situation with incomplete information, competing priorities, and more than one possible answer. Teams must understand the organization in the case, identify the decision that matters most, and build a recommendation that is both ambitious and workable.

The final pitch asks teams to explain their reasoning clearly. Strong work is not only creative; it connects evidence, financial thinking, customer understanding, operational reality, and a persuasive story.

Competition format

From first read to final pitch.

The experience is designed around the work participants do—not passive listening.

  1. Brief

    Read beyond the surface

    Map the facts, people, constraints, and goals. Decide which information changes the decision and which details are noise.

  2. Build

    Choose and test a direction

    Compare options, make reasonable assumptions, use business evidence, and turn the strongest choice into a practical plan.

  3. Pitch

    Make the recommendation clear

    Build a concise presentation, divide the story across the team, explain tradeoffs, and respond to questions with composure.

  4. Award

    Recognize strong thinking

    Prizes recognize teams that combine sound analysis, a feasible recommendation, effective teamwork, and persuasive communication.

Career exposure

See how business functions influence one another.

FIN

Finance

Use costs, returns, risk, and resource choices to judge whether an idea can hold up.

ACC

Accounting

Read the numbers carefully and connect performance information to a better decision.

MKT

Marketing

Understand the audience, value proposition, positioning, and route to meaningful demand.

ENT

Entrepreneurship

Spot opportunity, work with constraints, and turn an idea into a focused plan of action.

What participants develop

Practice the behaviours behind the job title.

A case competition makes skills visible. Participants must use them together, under a deadline, with other people depending on their contribution.

  • Problem framing
  • Business analysis
  • Team decision-making
  • Presentation design
  • Public speaking
  • Question response
  • Time management
  • Professional confidence
✦

Recognition

Prizes reward the quality of the thinking.

Award decisions consider how well teams understand the problem, support their recommendation, account for real-world constraints, and communicate as one team. Competition rules and prize details are provided to participants before competition day.
