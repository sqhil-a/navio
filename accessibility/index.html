Accessibility

Career information should be usable by more people.

Navio Pathways works to make its website, resources, communication, and online workshops clear and accessible.

Website and resource design

  • Keyboard navigation and visible focus indicators
  • Semantic headings, landmarks, labels, and status messages
  • Readable type, strong colour contrast, and responsive layouts
  • Support for browser zoom without horizontal scrolling at common widths
  • Reduced-motion preferences
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images
  • Core guidance available in HTML rather than only in a document download

Online workshops

Confirmed listings identify the online format and provide a way to request support. Depending on the session and available arrangements, support may include advance materials, additional written context, keyboard-accessible activities, captioning or transcript options, or another reasonable adjustment discussed with the participant.

You do not need to disclose a diagnosis. Describe the barrier or support that would help Navio plan an accessible experience. Please make the request as early as possible before a confirmed session.

Known limitations

External platforms, email services, and social networks may have accessibility limitations outside Navio’s direct control. If an external step creates a barrier, contact Navio so the team can discuss an alternative where practicable.

Feedback and accommodation requests

Email hello@naviopathways.com with “Accessibility request” in the subject line. Include the page or workshop involved, the barrier, the format or support that would help, and relevant timing. Do not include medical records.
