Website and resource terms

Clear expectations for public information and use.

These terms apply to the Navio Pathways website, self-guided career toolkit, public resources, and organizational contact routes.

General educational information

Website content and the career toolkit provide general educational information. They are not individualized academic, career, employment, legal, financial, or medical advice. Students should verify current admission, licensing, training, labour-market, and school requirements with the responsible institution or authority.

No guaranteed outcome

Using a resource or sending a message does not guarantee admission, employment, a placement, mentorship, certification, volunteer hours, salary, a guided activity, or another result.

Responsible use

Use the material for lawful educational purposes. Do not submit unnecessary sensitive information, harass another person, misrepresent Navio or its content, attempt to interfere with the website, or use an organizational contact route for promotion or solicitation.

Accuracy and updates

Navio reviews its own material periodically and publishes meaningful changes on the Updates page, but education routes, licensing rules, labour information, and external websites can change between reviews. A publication or review date does not make a page a substitute for current information from the responsible school, regulator, employer, training provider, or government source.

External sources and services

Career requirements and external information can change. Verify important decisions through current authoritative sources. Email, social media, linked websites, and other external services are governed by their own terms and availability; a link does not imply endorsement.

Intellectual property and use

You may use the public toolkit and guides for personal, family, or classroom learning with attribution to Navio Pathways. Do not present the content as your own, remove important qualifications, imply endorsement, or sell a copied version.

Questions

Questions about these terms can be sent to hello@naviopathways.com.
