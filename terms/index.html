Website and workshop terms

Clear expectations for public information and participation.

These terms apply to the Navio Pathways website, career resources, workshop listings, and registration workflow.

General educational information

Website content and workshops provide general educational information. They are not individualized academic, career, employment, legal, financial, or medical advice. Students should verify current admission, licensing, training, labour-market, and school requirements with the responsible institution or authority.

No guaranteed outcome

Using a resource, sending a message, registering, or attending does not guarantee admission, employment, a placement, mentorship, certification, volunteer hours, salary, or another result. Registration is not confirmed until Navio sends confirmation for a specific session.

Accurate registration

Provide accurate information, register only for yourself or with appropriate authority, and do not submit unnecessary sensitive information. Navio may decline or cancel a registration when a session is full, eligibility is not met, information is materially inaccurate, participation would create a safety concern, or the session changes.

Appropriate participation

Participants must treat others respectfully, follow moderator instructions, protect meeting access details, avoid recording or redistributing content without permission, and not use the session for harassment, promotion, solicitation, or unauthorized private contact. Navio may remove a participant to protect the group or the integrity of the activity.

Schedules and external services

Navio may change or cancel a session. The public workshop listing is the current source of schedule information, and registered participants will be contacted when practicable. Online delivery, email, social media, and any approved registration provider are also governed by those services’ terms and availability.

Intellectual property and use

You may use the public guides for personal, family, or classroom learning with attribution to Navio Pathways. Do not present the content as your own, remove important qualifications, imply endorsement, or sell a copied version.

Questions

Questions about these terms can be sent to hello@naviopathways.com.
