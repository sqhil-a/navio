Competition and website terms

Participate responsibly and follow the published rules.

These terms apply to NPCC registration, case materials, online participation, public website content, and organization contact routes.

Competition rules

Registered participants and advisors must follow the official NPCC 2026 rules, eligibility requirements, deadlines, presentation limits, conduct expectations, and technology-disclosure requirements.

Educational program

NPCC is a youth learning and competition program. Participation does not guarantee admission, employment, placement, school credit, volunteer hours, funding, a cash prize, or another outcome.

Case materials

The fictional case is provided for NPCC participation. Teams may quote and display the material in their competition deck with attribution. Do not sell, misleadingly alter, or publish the full case in a way that interferes with the competition.

External information

Teams are responsible for checking outside sources and identifying them in the deck. Navio is not responsible for changing content or services on third-party websites or platforms.

Responsible website use

Do not interfere with the website, impersonate another person, submit unnecessary sensitive information, misrepresent Navio content, or use contact routes for harassment, promotion, or solicitation.

Event administration

Navio may correct a clerical error, address accessibility or safety, and adjust event timing when technology or circumstances require it. Material changes are published on the competition page and sent to registered advisors.
