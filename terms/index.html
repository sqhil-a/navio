Program and website terms

Use the material responsibly and verify important facts.

These terms apply to the Career Clarity Program, workbook, public resources, website, and organization contact routes.

Educational purpose

The program provides general career education. It is not individualized academic, career, employment, legal, financial, or medical advice.

No guaranteed outcome

Participation does not guarantee admission, employment, placement, certification, volunteer hours, salary, or another outcome. Navio does not issue school credit or a credential for the self-guided program.

Current information

Education routes, licensing rules, labour information, and external sites can change. Verify important decisions through the responsible school, regulator, employer, training provider, or government source.

Responsible use

Do not misrepresent Navio content, remove important qualifications, interfere with the website, submit unnecessary sensitive information, or use contact routes for harassment, promotion, or solicitation.

Reuse

You may use the program and workbook for personal, family, or classroom learning with attribution to Navio Pathways. Do not sell a copied version or imply Navio endorsement.
