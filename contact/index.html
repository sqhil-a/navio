Response information

Include only what is needed.

State the page or program stage involved, your question, and any relevant timing. Do not send medical records, government identifiers, financial information, passwords, or unnecessary sensitive information about a young person.

Email is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.
