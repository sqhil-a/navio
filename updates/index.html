Public updates

Current information, with dates and clear status.

This page records meaningful changes to Navio Pathways programs, resources, and public organization information.

August 12, 2026 · Program update

Self-guided career toolkit is publicly available.

Navio Pathways has published a free six-part toolkit for Ontario secondary students. It covers choosing an exploration question, describing daily work, comparing pathways, preparing professional questions, testing an interest, and reflecting on evidence.

The toolkit is available in HTML without an account, application, or collection of student responses. Educators and community organizations may separately inquire about guided group use.

Use the career toolkit →

August 12, 2026 · Resource update

Three original career guides are available.

Students, families, and educators can now use expanded HTML guides for researching a career, preparing a professional conversation, and reflecting on a career decision. The resources are available without an account or download.

Use the career guides →

August 10, 2026 · Organization update

Registered address added to public contact information.

Navio Pathways added its registered address—3140 Polo Place, Mississauga, Ontario—to the website footer and contact information so the organization’s public identity is easier to verify. Its Ontario Corporation Number remains 1001662092.

How this page is maintained.

Navio publishes an update when a program opens or closes, a substantial resource is added, or public organizational information changes. Historical updates remain visible unless they contain information that should no longer be public.

The career-toolkit page is the source of truth for the current self-guided program.
